# Business Use Case

## Industry Context

Industrial suppliers and distributors often receive inquiries from multiple fragmented channels such as IndiaMART, WhatsApp, emails, and direct calls.

Sales teams manually:
- extract inquiry details
- identify missing information
- coordinate supplier pricing
- prepare quotations
- generate PDFs
- send follow-ups

This creates operational inefficiencies and delayed response times.

## Core Operational Problems

Common operational issues include:

- repetitive manual quotation preparation
- inconsistent quotation formats
- delayed customer response times
- lack of centralized tracking
- dependency on individual sales executives
- missed follow-ups
- difficulty handling multiple supplier quotations

## Business Impact

Slow quotation workflows can directly impact:
- customer conversion rates
- operational efficiency
- sales productivity
- response turnaround time

In competitive industrial supply markets, faster response times often improve the probability of winning customer orders.

## Proposed Solution

QuoteFlow AI is an AI assisted quotation operations workflow designed to automate repetitive quotation handling tasks while keeping humans involved in customer communication and approval decisions.

The workflow supports:
- inquiry extraction
- missing information detection
- sales call preparation
- quotation generation
- branded PDF creation
- approval workflows
- operational tracking

## Workflow Overview

1. Inquiry received from email or IndiaMART
2. AI extracts structured inquiry data
3. Missing information identified
4. Internal sales call summary generated
5. Human approval checkpoint
6. Supplier pricing logic applied
7. Quotation PDF generated
8. Customer quotation email sent
9. Workflow logged for tracking

## Target Customers

Primary target users include:
- electrical distributors
- industrial suppliers
- machinery vendors
- procurement heavy SMEs
- pipe and valve distributors
