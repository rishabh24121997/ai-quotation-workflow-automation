# QuoteFlow AI

AI Assisted Quotation Operations Workflow for Industrial Suppliers and Distributors

## Problem Statement

Industrial suppliers and distributors often receive quotation inquiries through fragmented channels such as IndiaMART, WhatsApp, emails, and phone calls.

Sales teams spend significant manual effort:
- extracting inquiry details
- clarifying missing information
- coordinating supplier pricing
- preparing quotations
- generating PDFs
- tracking approvals and follow-ups

This leads to delayed response times and operational inefficiencies.

## Solution

QuoteFlow AI is an AI assisted quotation operations workflow designed to streamline quotation handling for industrial SMEs.

The system automates repetitive operational tasks while keeping humans in control of approvals and customer interactions.

## Features

- Inquiry extraction from unstructured emails/messages
- Structured JSON generation
- Missing information detection
- Internal sales call preparation summaries
- Human approval checkpoints
- Supplier and pricing workflow logic
- Branded PDF quotation generation
- Automated email delivery
- Google Sheets CRM logging
- Workflow status tracking
- Error handling and fallback notifications

## Business Use Case

Target Users:
- industrial suppliers
- electrical distributors
- procurement-heavy SMEs
- pipe and valve suppliers

Primary Goal:
Reduce quotation turnaround time from hours to minutes while improving operational visibility and sales preparedness.
